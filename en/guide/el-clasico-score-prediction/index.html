Predict the score of El Clásico

El Clásico is the most watched match in Spain and one of the biggest in the world. When it is on, it is usually the match of the day on Daily Score: everyone predicts the same match and the debate starts long before kick-off.

Why El Clásico is so hard to predict

Form often counts less than usual in this match: the pressure, what is at stake in the title race and the quality of both squads make surprises frequent. Look at the last head-to-head results and at who plays at home.

Which score to pick

In big matches, close scores like 1-1, 2-1 or 1-2 are common, but El Clásico has also produced heavy wins. An exact score earns 3 points and the right result 1 point: a bold pick can cost you or pay off big.
