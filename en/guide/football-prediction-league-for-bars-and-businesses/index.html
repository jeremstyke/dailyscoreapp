A football prediction league for your bar, club or business

Daily Score lets any bar, sports club, shop, company or creator run its own public football prediction league for free. Your customers or fans predict the score of the big match of the day, climb your ranking, and see your logo, your website and your offers every time they play.

Why create a league for your business?

Football brings people together, and a prediction league gives them a reason to come back every day. Your league keeps your customers or your community engaged between two visits, creates friendly rivalry on match nights, and puts your brand in front of them every time they check the ranking. It costs nothing to set up and runs by itself: the match of the day, the scores and the ranking are handled automatically.

Who is it for?

Bars and pubs that show football matches, sports clubs and supporters' groups, shops and brands linked to football, companies looking for a simple team game, and creators or influencers who want to animate their community. Your league can be played worldwide or in one country, and players can join in one tap from the list of public leagues.

What your league can show

Your league has its own name and description, a photo or logo, and a button to visit your website. As the creator, you can pin a message at the top of the league chat: the rules, the prize for the winner, a happy hour on match nights or a discount for members. Members get a live ranking of the league, with their points updated after every match.

How to create your league in 2 minutes

Open Daily Score, go to the Leagues tab and tap Create a league. Choose Public, give it a name and a description, then choose where it is played (the world or your country), a start date and a duration from 7 days to 1 year. Add your photo or logo and your website, then share the league with your customers: on your social networks, on a poster with a QR code in your bar, or in your newsletter. Public leagues are quickly checked before they appear in the list.

How much does it cost?

Creating a public league is free. The photo or logo and the website button are free during the launch season; later, they will become paid options for new leagues only, and leagues already created keep them. There is no betting and no real money in Daily Score: players play for fun, points and rankings.

Ideas to make your league a success

Announce the league on your social networks and in your venue, and offer a small prize to the winner of the month (a drink, a discount, a shirt). Post the ranking on match nights, congratulate the best predictor of the week, and use the pinned message to remind members of your next event. A simple QR code to your league on the tables is often enough to get the first players. Any prize you offer should be free to enter, with no purchase needed to play.
