Daily Score rules: points, rankings and tie-breaks

Daily Score takes 10 seconds a day, but a few simple rules make the rankings exciting. Here is everything you need to know.

The match of the day

Every day, one big match is offered to all players. You make your score prediction before kick-off, and you can change it until the match starts.

How points work

Exact score: 3 points. Right result (the right winner or a draw): 1 point. Wrong result: 0 points. The score at the end of normal time is what counts.

The rankings

You appear in the world ranking, your country ranking, your friends ranking and each of your leagues, by day, week, month and all time.

How ties are broken

When players have the same points, they are separated in this order: most exact scores, then the most accurate predictions (smallest goal gap with the real scores), then who predicted earliest before kick-off.

Streaks and badges

Predict several days in a row to build your streak and unlock badges. A reminder is sent before each match so you never forget.
