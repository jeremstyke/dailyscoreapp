Free football prediction game with friends: how it works

Predicting matches with your mates is even more fun with a ranking and a bit of banter. Here is how to play for free with your friends on Daily Score.

One match a day, for everyone

Every day, Daily Score picks the big match not to miss (Champions League, Premier League, La Liga, Serie A, Bundesliga, Ligue 1…). Everyone predicts the same match, which makes comparing easy and debates lively. You can also vote for tomorrow's match.

Create your private league in one tap

In the Leagues tab, create your league, choose its name and emoji, then share the invite link with your friends. Each league has its own ranking and a chat wall to talk about the matches.

Challenge your friends directly

After your prediction, the Challenge my friends button sends your score to a friend in the game, or to anyone on Telegram. Perfect to get the debate going before kick-off.

Follow the match live

During the match, the score is shown live in the game, with goal alerts. After the final whistle, you get your result and your ranking.

Free and no betting

Daily Score is a game just for fun: no stakes, no odds, no money to win. Points are only used for the rankings. Play in your browser, on Telegram or with the Android app.
