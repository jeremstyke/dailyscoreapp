Public leagues on Daily Score: the complete guide

Public leagues let you play against football fans from all over the world, or from your country, without needing an invitation code. Here is everything you need to know to join one or create your own.

What is a public league?

A public league is a league that everyone can see and join. It appears in the Leagues tab, under Public leagues, either in the World list or in the list of a country. Each league has its own ranking, based on the predictions its members make every day during the league's dates.

How to join a public league

Open Daily Score, go to the Leagues tab and tap Public leagues. Choose World or your country, then tap View to see the description, the dates, the members and the ranking. If you like it, tap Join: you are in, and your next predictions count for that league too. You can belong to several leagues at the same time.

How to create your own public league

In the Leagues tab, tap Create a league and choose Public. Give it a name and a description, then choose where it is played: the whole world or one country. Pick a start date (up to two months ahead) and a duration: 7 days, 30 days, the current month, 3 months or 1 year. Public leagues created by players are quickly checked before they appear in the list, so that names and descriptions stay friendly for everyone.

Photo, website and pinned message

As the creator, you can give your league a photo or a logo, and add a website: perfect for bars, clubs, shops or creators who want to bring their community together. You can also pin a message at the top of the league chat, for the rules, the prize or an announcement. Public leagues are free. The photo and the website are free during the launch season; later, they will become paid options for new leagues only, and leagues already created keep them.

The Daily Score World League

The first public league, the Daily Score World League, starts on October 9 and lasts one full year. It is open to everyone: join it now, predict the match of the day every day and try to become the first world champion of Daily Score.

Tips to climb your league ranking

Predict every day, even when the match looks hard: a right result still earns 1 point, and an exact score 3 points. When players are tied, the one with the most exact scores, then the most accurate predictions, then the earliest predictions is ranked first. And keep an eye on the league chat: it is the best place to share predictions and tease your rivals.
