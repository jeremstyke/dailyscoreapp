How to create a private football prediction league with friends

Playing against the whole world is fun, but beating your friends is even better. On Daily Score, a private league takes a few seconds to create and it is completely free.

Step 1: open the Leagues tab

Open Daily Score in your browser, on Telegram or in the Android app, then tap the Leagues tab at the bottom of the screen.

Step 2: create your league

Tap Create a league, choose a name (for example "The Sunday crew") and an emoji. Your league is ready instantly, with its own invitation code.

Step 3: invite your friends

Tap Invite to share the link in your group chat. Your friends join in one tap, and they are automatically added as your friends in the game.

Step 4: predict and follow your ranking

Every day, everyone predicts the same big match. Your league has its own ranking by day, week, month and all time, and ties are broken by exact scores, accuracy and speed.

Bonus: the league wall

Each league has a chat wall to comment on the matches, celebrate an exact score or tease the friend who got it all wrong. You can mute its notifications at any time.
