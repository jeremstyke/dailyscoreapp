How does it work?

  1. Every day, Daily Score picks the most anticipated match (or you vote for tomorrow's).
  2. You make your score prediction before kick-off.
  3. Exact score = 3 points, right result (win or draw) = 1 point.
  4. Follow the match live, then get your result and your ranking.

Which competitions?

The biggest matches from the Premier League, La Liga, Serie A, Bundesliga, Ligue 1, Champions League, Eredivisie, Liga Portugal and Brasileirão, plus international fixtures like the Nations League during international breaks.

Frequently asked questions

Is Daily Score really free?

Yes. The game is completely free. A Premium option exists for players who want to support the game and unlock the daily trend and AI prediction, with no ads.

Is it a betting site?

No. Daily Score is a prediction game just for fun: no stakes, no odds and no money to win. Points are only used for the rankings.

How are points calculated?

An exact score earns 3 points, the right result (win or draw) earns 1 point. Ties are broken by the number of exact scores, prediction accuracy, then how early players made their picks.

Can I play with my friends?

Yes: create a private league, share the invite link, and follow your own ranking, with a chat wall for each league.

Where can I play?

In your browser, on Telegram or with the Android app. Your progress is the same everywhere.
