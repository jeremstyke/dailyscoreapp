Challenge the Daily Score AI with Your Friends!

Daily Score now has an AI opponent. Add it as a friend or invite it to your league and see if you can outsmart it every day.

Daily Score just got a new challenger: the Daily Score AI. It predicts every match, earns points just like you do, and shows up right in your rankings. Can you beat it?

Adding the AI as a friend is instant. Search for Daily Score AI in the friends section, send the request, and it accepts straight away. From that moment it appears in your friends ranking so you can compare your predictions head to head every day.

League creators can also bring the AI into any league they run. Open your league, tap Invite the AI, and it joins immediately. It will predict every match and be ranked alongside all the members, making every round a little more competitive.

Whether you play solo or in a group, the AI is a fun new benchmark. Exact score gets 3 points, correct result gets 1 point, and the AI plays by the same rules. Outsmarting it on a tricky match feels great.
