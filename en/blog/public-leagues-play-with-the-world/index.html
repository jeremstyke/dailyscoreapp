Public Leagues Are Here: Play With the World!

Daily Score just launched public leagues. Join a world or country league in one tap, or create your own. Free during launch season!

Big news for Daily Score players: public leagues have arrived, and they make the game more fun than ever. You can now compete not just with friends but with players from all over the world or from your own country.

Joining is simple. Head to the Leagues tab, tap Public Leagues, and pick the one that suits you. No fuss, no friction, just more competition.

Want to run your own public league? You can create one with a title, a description, a world or country focus, a start date, and a duration anywhere from 7 days to a full year. Add a photo or logo to make it stand out, and if you represent a brand, a bar or a club, you can even display your website. As the creator, you can also pin a message at the top of the league chat to keep everyone in the loop.

To kick things off, the Daily Score World League starts on October 9 and runs for one year. It is the perfect place to test yourself against players everywhere.

Best of all, public leagues, photos and website links are completely free during the launch season. Enjoy every feature while it lasts, then watch this space for what comes next!
