## How it works

1. Every day, Daily Score picks **the most anticipated match** (Champions League, Premier League, La Liga, Serie A, Bundesliga, Ligue 1...). Players can also vote for tomorrow's match.
2. You make your **score prediction** before kick-off.
3. **Exact score = 3 points**, right result (win or draw) = 1 point.
4. Follow the match **live**, then get your result and your ranking.

## Features

- 🏆 **Rankings**: world, country and friends, by day, week, month and all time, with tie-breaks (exact scores, accuracy, speed)
- 👥 **Private leagues** with a chat wall, invitations and notifications
- ⚔️ **Challenges**: challenge your friends on today's match, in private or in any Telegram chat
- 📡 **Live score** with goal alerts, and the full detail of past matches (how everyone predicted, who found the exact score)
- 🤖 **Trend and AI prediction**: what everyone picked and the AI's pick for the match
- 🔥 **Daily streaks**, badges and reminders before each match
- 💬 **Telegram groups**: the bot posts results and the group ranking after each match
- 🌍 **9 languages**: English, French, Spanish, Portuguese, German, Italian, Arabic, Turkish, Russian

## About this repository

This repository hosts the public website **dailyscoreapp.com** (GitHub Pages): the game page, the Android app download, and the multilingual guides and match pages, which are published automatically every day.

Daily Score is a free game for fun: there are no stakes, no odds and no money to win. Points are only used for the rankings.
