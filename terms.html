The game

Every day, you can predict the score of one football match. Points, rankings, streaks, badges and leagues are for fun only. Daily Score is not a betting service: you cannot bet or win money or prizes.

Purchases

Some optional extras can be bought with Telegram Stars on Telegram. They give no points and no advantage in the rankings.

Match data

Matches and results come from external data providers. In case of an error, a postponed or cancelled match, points may be corrected or not awarded.
