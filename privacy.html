Daily Score - Privacy policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026. Daily Score is a free football prediction game made by Jeremy (@jeremstyke), available on the web, on Telegram and on Android.

What we collect

When you sign in, we receive your public profile from Google (account id, first name, profile picture), GitHub (id, username, display name, avatar) or Telegram (id, first name, language). In the game we store your chosen language and country, your predictions, points, streak, badges, leagues and the friends you invited. We do not collect your email, your contacts, your location or any payment card data.

How we use it

Only to run the game: show your predictions, compute points and rankings (your first name, country and points are visible to other players in the rankings and in your leagues), and, on Telegram, send you match reminders and results. We do not sell or share your data with advertisers.

Payments

Optional purchases (Premium, tips, trend) are handled by Telegram (Telegram Stars) inside Telegram, and by Gumroad on the website and the Android app (card, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay). Gumroad processes the payment and only tells us that the purchase was made (with the email you gave Gumroad, used to activate your Premium). We never see your card or bank details. See Gumroad's privacy policy.

Storage and deletion

Data is stored on secure servers (Supabase, Railway). To delete your account and all your data, write to @jeremstyke on Telegram or to juryjeremy@gmail.com: it will be done within 30 days.

Children

Daily Score is not intended for children under 13.
